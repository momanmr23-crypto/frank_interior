01 — Who we are

We design, build and finish interiors — with people at the centre and purpose at every turn.

Frank Interior Contractor is a process-driven interior studio for homes and commercial spaces. Fit-outs, renovations, custom joinery and finishing work — planned openly, managed tightly, handed over clean.

02 — Our process

Clear process. Creative results.

Human centred.
Always curious.

  1. 01

    Consult

    A free site visit, an honest conversation and a clear brief — scope, budget and timeline on the table from day one.

  2. 02

    Plan

    Designs, material boards and a transparent estimate. Every decision agreed before a single wall moves.

  3. 03

    Build

    Skilled crews, daily supervision and tidy sites. Progress you can see, updates you never have to chase.

  4. 04

    Handover

    Snag-free finishing, a full clean and aftercare support. We don't leave until it's right.

At Frank, the process is the product: open, honest and relentlessly tidy — from the first call through the final coat.
