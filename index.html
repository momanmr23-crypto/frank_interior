How it works

Four steps, no surprises

A simple, repeatable process so you always know what happens next and what it will cost.

  1. 01

    Enquiry & site visit

    You get in touch with a rough idea of scope and budget. I visit the space, measure properly and talk through what you want.

  2. 02

    Fixed written quote

    You receive an itemised quote covering labour, materials and finish schedule — plus any options to spend or save.

  3. 03

    Build & weekly updates

    Work is scheduled around your life or your trading hours, with a short update at the end of every week.

  4. 04

    Handover & snag list

    We walk the space together, fix the small things and hand over finishes, care notes and warranties.
